About placements cell

The Placement Cell of Sri Venkateswara College is a student-run body dedicated to fostering professional growth and career opportunities for the student community. Established with the vision of bridging the gap between academia and industry, the Placement Cell actively works towards ensuring that every student has access to meaningful internship and placement opportunities. The cell organises recruitment drives, skill-development workshops, and networking events in collaboration with leading companies across sectors — including finance, consulting, technology, media, and public policy. With a team of over 250 students and a dedicated faculty convener, SVC's Placement Cell operates with the professionalism, rigour, and dedication of a full-fledged career services office.
