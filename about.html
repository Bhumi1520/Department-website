About us

The Department of Life Science at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, is a multidisciplinary academic unit that integrates the core disciplines of Botany, Chemistry, and Zoology. Established in 1973, the department provides a strong foundation in the biological sciences through an interdisciplinary approach. The department offers the B.Sc. (Programme) Life Science, designed to equip students with conceptual clarity, practical skills, and analytical thinking across molecular, cellular, organismal, and environmental levels of life. Through classroom teaching, laboratory training, and academic mentoring, the department fosters scientific temper, curiosity, and a spirit of inquiry among students.
